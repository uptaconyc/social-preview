Style options

The same 13 posts as a profile grid, in six looks. Pick a look to switch every post at once.

  • Film: warm, faded, coarse grain, vignette. The original look.
  • Clean: true color, bright, a little extra saturation, crisp, no grain.
  • Lilac: clean and bright with the brand lilac in the shadows.
  • Flash: hard contrast, bright subject, dark edges, crisp detail. Video runs at 15 frames per second for a choppy snapshot feel. Turns steak grey-purple.
  • Flash warm: same punch with a warm white balance. Eggs read golden, tortillas toasted, steak red-brown.
  • Flash warm + bright: warm, about 10% brighter, highlights protected so eggs and tortillas keep texture, softer vignette, slight shadow lift for dark beef.
  • Flash natural: the Flash contrast and 15 fps video, warm, no vignette, half the sharpening, gentler saturation so skin stays natural, shadow detail kept for dark drinks and beef. Recommended.
